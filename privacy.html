🔒 Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 27, 2026

Math Practice (also called Math Buddy) is a math practice app for children in kindergarten through 5th grade. It is made by an independent developer (Kranthi Alapati, GitHub: kranthi-rigas). This policy explains what happens to information in the app. The short version: we do not collect any information at all.

No data collection

  • The app does not collect, send, sell or share any personal information or usage data.
  • There are no accounts, no sign-in, and no email or phone number is ever asked for.
  • There is no advertising, no analytics, no tracking and no third-party advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs.
  • There are no in-app purchases, no chat and no social features.
  • The app does not use the camera, microphone, contacts, photos or location.

Information stored only on your device

To remember progress, the app saves a few things only on your device (in the app's local storage): each child's first name or nickname and avatar, grade, scores, stars, stickers, badges, practice history, questions to review, settings, and the parent PIN. The app never sends this information to us or to anyone else. (If you have turned on your device's own backup, such as iCloud or Google backup, it may be included in that private backup, which you control.) You can delete a child's profile in the parent dashboard at any time, and uninstalling the app (or clearing this website's data in your browser) removes everything.

Read-aloud

The optional read-aloud feature uses the text-to-speech engine that is built into your device. The app only hands the text of the math question to that system speech engine; the app itself does not record or send anything.

Internet

The app works fully offline. It does not load ads or other content from the internet and does not contain links to websites or other apps for children. Grown-up information (such as this policy's web address) is only shown inside the PIN-protected parent area.

Children's privacy

The app is designed for children and is intended to comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), GDPR-K and the Apple App Store Kids Category and Google Play Families policies. Because no personal information is collected from anyone, there is nothing for us to access, share or delete on our side.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted at the same address with a new effective date. We will not start collecting data without updating this policy first.

Contact

Questions? Contact the developer: open an issue at github.com/kranthi-rigas/math-practice/issues (GitHub profile: kranthi-rigas).
